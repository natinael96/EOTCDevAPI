Bahire Hasab ባሕረ ሐሳብ

"The sea of thought": the traditional computation that locates every movable fast and feast of the church year. The API exposes the whole calculation, not just the answers, so you can check its work.

GET/v1/bahire-hasab/{year}
$ curl …/v1/bahire-hasab/2018
{
  "ethiopicYear": 2018,
  "ameteAlem": 7518,
  "evangelist": { … },
  "computation": {
    "medeb": 18,
    "wenber": 17,
    "abekte": 12,
    "metqi": 18,
    "mebajaHamer": { "ethiopic": "2018-…", "weekday": { … } },
    "tewsakApplied": 0
  },
  "newYear": { "gregorian": "2025-09-11", "isLeapYear": false, "pagumenDays": 5 },
  "movableFeasts": [ … ]
}

The traditional chain¶

Amete Alem (ዓመተ ዓለም, years since creation, EC + 5500)
  → Medeb (መደብ)
  → Wenber (ወንበር)
  → Abekte & Metqi (አበቅቴ · መጥቅዕ)
  → Mebaja Hamer (መባጃ ሐመር)
  → weekday + Tewsak (ተውሳክ)
  → ጾመ ነነዌ (the Nineveh fast, the linchpin)
  → every other movable feast

Nineveh is the anchor. Once its Monday is fixed, all ten remaining movable feasts sit a fixed Tewsak offset after it: Great Lent begins 14 days later, Hosanna 62, Siklet 67, Fasika 69, Erget 108, Peraklitos 118. Each feast in movableFeasts includes its offset from Nineveh so the structure is visible, not just the result.

Invariants the tests enforce¶

  • Abekte + Metqi = 30 for all 401 verified years;
  • Nineveh always lands on a Monday; Fasika and Hosanna on a Sunday; Siklet on a Friday;
  • Fasika always falls within its canonical April 4 – May 8 window;
  • 73,415 consecutive days round-trip EC → JDN → EC with no gaps or duplicates;
  • weekdays cross-checked against an independent implementation.

The correctness argument¶

Fasika is computed two completely independent ways: the traditional Bahire Hasab chain above, and the Alexandrian computus (Meeus' Julian-calendar algorithm). The test suite asserts they agree for every year from 1800 to 2200 EC. Two methods with nothing in common but the answer, agreeing for four centuries, is not a coincidence; it is the strongest guarantee this project can offer without a church-court ruling.

The evangelist of the year (ወንጌላዊ: Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John, cycling with Amete Alem mod 4) is included in the response for completeness; the new-year block states whether the year is a leap year (Year of Luke, year mod 4 = 3) and therefore whether Pagumen has 5 or 6 days.
